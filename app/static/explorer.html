    GP-150 confirmed working

    Upload a GP-150 .prst file, or read one live off the pedal over WebMIDI, to see its patch name, signal chain, and every module's real model, parameters, and on/off state. A live read also shows controls for editing parameter values, toggling modules on/off, swapping a module's model, drag-reordering the chain, and saving the result to a slot — all confirmed working on real hardware (2026-09-20). GP-150's container has no model records or bypass mask (a different format from GP-5/GP-50), so it can't go through the inventory above and gets its own view here instead — see design/GP150_SUPPORT.md for exactly what's confirmed and where.
